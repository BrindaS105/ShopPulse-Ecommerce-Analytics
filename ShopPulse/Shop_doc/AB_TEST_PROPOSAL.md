# A/B Test Proposal – ShopPulse

## Experiment Name
Product Recommendation Placement Test

## Objective
Evaluate whether showing related product recommendations on a
product page improves the purchase conversion rate.

## Hypothesis
Customers who see relevant product recommendations may be more
likely to add an additional product to their cart.

## Variants

### Control Group
Customers see the existing product page without the new
recommendation section.

### Treatment Group
Customers see a product recommendation section below the
main product information.

## Primary Metric
Purchase conversion rate.

Formula:

Number of users who purchase / Number of users exposed to the page × 100

## Secondary Metrics
- Add-to-cart rate
- Average order value
- Click-through rate on recommendations

## Experiment Considerations
- Randomly assign eligible users to groups.
- Keep the experience consistent during the test.
- Collect enough observations before evaluating results.
- Check whether the difference is statistically meaningful.

## Success Criteria
The treatment should improve the primary metric without creating
a meaningful negative effect on other important user outcomes.

## Status
This is a proposed experiment. It has not been conducted.
