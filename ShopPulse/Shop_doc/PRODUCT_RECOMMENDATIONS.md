# Product Recommendations – ShopPulse

The following are proposed actions based on the sample dataset.

## 1. Review High-Revenue Products
Keyboard and Desk Lamp generate the highest product revenue.
The business could review their availability, pricing and customer demand.

## 2. Maintain Popular Products
Notebook has the highest unit sales.
The business could monitor inventory and avoid stock shortages.

## 3. Explore Customer Retention
Six customers made more than one purchase.
A future analysis could examine their purchase frequency and preferences.

## 4. Investigate Monthly Trends
June has the highest revenue in the sample.
The business could investigate whether product mix, order quantity
or other factors contributed to the increase.

## 5. Improve Analytics
A larger dataset could support:
- Customer segmentation
- Cohort analysis
- Product recommendations
- Promotion analysis
- Sales forecasting

## Limitations
These are hypotheses and proposed actions based on a small synthetic
dataset. They should be validated using real business data before
making business decisions.
