# Metrics Definition – ShopPulse

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Total Orders | Count of all order records |
| Total Revenue | Sum of quantity multiplied by unit price |
| Average Order Value | Total revenue divided by number of orders |
| Unique Customers | Count of distinct customer IDs |
| Units Sold | Sum of quantities |
| Repeat Customers | Customers with more than one order |
| Category Revenue | Revenue grouped by product category |
| Product Revenue | Revenue grouped by product |
| Monthly Revenue | Revenue grouped by calendar month |

## Important Formulas

Revenue:

Quantity × Unit Price

Average Order Value:

Total Revenue ÷ Total Orders

Repeat Customer:

A customer with more than one order in the dataset.

## Measurement Limitations
The dataset contains only 20 orders. These metrics are useful
for demonstrating SQL analysis but are not sufficient to make
claims about a real e-commerce business.
