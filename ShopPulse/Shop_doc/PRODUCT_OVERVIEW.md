# Product Overview – ShopPulse

## Product Concept
ShopPulse is a product analytics exercise designed to convert
transaction data into business insights.

## Problem Statement
E-commerce businesses need visibility into product performance,
revenue trends and customer purchasing patterns.

## Target Users
- E-commerce business owners
- Product managers
- Sales analysts
- Business analysts

## Main Questions
1. How much revenue does the business generate?
2. Which products sell the most?
3. Which category contributes the most revenue?
4. How does monthly revenue change?
5. How many customers purchase more than once?

## Proposed Solution
Use SQL queries to summarize transaction records and identify
patterns that can inform product and business decisions.

## Scope
This project focuses on descriptive analysis of a small,
synthetic sales dataset.
